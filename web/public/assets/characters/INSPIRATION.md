# Character Art 2.1 provenance

The six bundled PNG cutouts are newly generated original concepts made for Abyss Expedition. No third-party raster artwork is redistributed or used as an image-to-image input.

Research references used only for production conventions, silhouette readability, shared anchors, and commercial-license checks:

- willYEE, “Dark Fantasy Pixel Pack — 54 Characters, 25 Monsters, 455 Weapons” (CC0): https://willyee.itch.io/dark-fantasy-pixel-pack-54-characters-25-monsters-455-weapons
- Duncan Hall, “Stock Art — Grimdark Fantasy Characters” (CC0): https://nacnudllah.itch.io/stock-art-grimdark-fantasy-characters
- comphonia, “Dark Knight 2D Character Sprites” (CC0): https://opengameart.org/content/dark-knight-2d-character-sprites
- Pipoya free-material index and usage terms, reviewed for format conventions only: https://pipoya.net/sozai/

Art direction: mature painted graphic-novel dark fantasy; charcoal iron, bone, muted emerald and restrained crimson; three-quarter profile facing right; transparent background and bottom-center visual anchor.

Character identities:

- Warrior — Iron Vow: broad plate silhouette and chipped greatsword.
- Mage — Echo Scholar: high collar, staff and orbiting arcane shards.
- Ranger — Verdant Tracker: hood, recurved bow and toxin bottles.
- Paladin — Last Lantern: ivory shield-wall silhouette and mace.
- Necromancer — Pale Choir: bone mantle, caged soul lantern and spectral hand.
- Creator — Unwritten: porcelain half-mask, reality blade and broken geometric rings.
