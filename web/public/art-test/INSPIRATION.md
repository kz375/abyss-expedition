# Art FX Lab 0.3 visual research

The shipped UI and effects are original HTML/CSS/JavaScript drawings. No third-party raster asset is bundled.

- KRYN STUDIO, *Ultimate Soulslike UI Kit* (CC0): blood-red and abyss-blue HUD hierarchy, modular framed panels. https://itch.io/t/6067754/
- StumpyStrust, *Fantasy UI Box* (CC0): restrained demonic-red framing and dark inset surfaces. https://opengameart.org/content/fantasy-ui-box
- Cethiel, *Weapon Slash - Effect* (CC0): multi-arc weapon trails, color variants and impact timing. https://opengameart.org/content/weapon-slash-effect
- Mikodrak, *2D Spell Effects* (CC0): layered core, particles and dissipating outer wave. https://opengameart.org/content/2d-spell-effects
- ぴぽや: animation readability, frame pacing and effect silhouette research. Its free-asset terms allow game use and modification; no Pipoya asset file is redistributed here. https://pipoya.net/sozai/terms-of-use/

These links document inspiration and licensing provenance; they do not imply endorsement by the original artists.
